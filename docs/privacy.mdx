---
title: 🔒 Privacy and security
description: What tutti keeps on the phone, how it trusts the devices, and what it never does.
---

tutti only talks to devices on the local network, and keeps everything it needs on the phone.

## Local network only

Every connection goes to a private LAN address. Plain HTTP is refused for anything else.

## Trust on first use

The TV's and the bar's certificates are pinned the first time tutti meets them. A changed certificate is refused rather than silently accepted, and TLS verification is never switched off.

## Secrets on the phone

The pairing material, the Pre-Shared Key and any Sony tokens are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and excluded from backups. "Forget device" wipes the pairing; signing out of Sony wipes the tokens.

## Sony account

Sony sign-in is optional. When used, it goes through OAuth with PKCE in a Custom Tab: tutti never sees the password and never uses a WebView for it.

## Developer mode

With developer mode on, errors show their technical details with a Copy button. Addresses, keys and tokens are redacted before anything is shown or copied.
