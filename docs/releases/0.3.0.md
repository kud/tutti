# tutti 0.3.0

Search from the playback controls, sharper lock-screen artwork and Settings that show how things stand.

## New

- **Search without leaving the remote**. From the playback controls, search inside the app on screen or across the whole TV. YouTube has its own search shortcut too.
- **Artwork on the lock screen**. The lock-screen card leads with the artwork of what’s playing, sharp rather than a blurred app icon.
- **Settings at a glance**. Settings opens on four tiles: TV, soundbar, sync and features. Anything that needs you comes first, with the one button that sorts it.
- **Edit the dock with a long press**. Press and hold an empty spot in the dock to rearrange it.
- **More app icons**. Many UK and European TV apps now wear their own icons, from BBC iPlayer to ZDF, DAZN and Rakuten TV.
- **Stable or test builds**. In About, choose whether to get test builds. With them off, tutti stays on final releases. _(GitHub builds, which update themselves.)_

## Better

- **The TV’s link, as it is**. The remote says connected only when it is, and shows a drop straight away. Controls the TV can’t take just now dim quietly.
- **One way to say how things stand**. Connections, Settings and setup use the same words and signs for each device’s state.
- **Back to the remote after an update**. An update closes tutti. A quiet notification now brings you straight back. _(GitHub builds, which update themselves.)_
- **Nuvio’s fast forward on the jog wheel**. Over Nuvio, the jog wheel jumps by the app’s own step.

## Fixed

- **Keys no longer vanish after the phone sleeps**. A TV connection that went quiet while the phone slept is reopened before your first press goes into it.
- **Playback controls no longer crash over Nuvio**. Opening them over Nuvio could close tutti. Not any more.
- **The TV’s own apps, by name**. The now-showing card names the TV’s built-in apps too, not only the ones tutti already knows.
