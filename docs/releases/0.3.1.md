# tutti 0.3.1

Say again? and an end-of-episode switch-off in Playback, a pause for phone calls, and Settings with a page for each topic.

## New

- **Say again?** In Playback, Say again? goes back a few seconds and turns on captions and the bar’s voice enhancement until the line has played again, then puts both back.
- **Switch off at the end of the episode**. From Power or Playback, the TV and the soundbar switch off when what you cast ends, fading the sound over its last two minutes. A switch-off can also wait for a time of day.
- **A pause for phone calls**. In Settings › Automatic, the TV can pause when the phone rings or a call starts, and play again once the call ends.
- **Quieter evenings**. Between two times you choose, the soundbar switches to Night and Voice and keeps a lower volume guard, then everything goes back.
- **Picture mode by app**. The TV’s picture mode can change with the app or input on screen.
- **Share what’s on**. Share the title playing, with its cover art. Optional buttons open it on Trakt, JustWatch or IMDb.
- **Title details**. If you turn it on, Playback shows the synopsis, cast and rating of what’s playing, from Cinemeta.
- **Open an app where you want**. A dock pin can open its app at a page or link you choose, rather than where it was last time.
- **Back up your settings**. Save your settings to a file and bring them back. Devices, addresses and keys are never in it.

## Better

- **A page for each topic**. Settings has one page per topic, Automatic and Say again? included, and keeps your place when you come back.
- **Third-party services in one place**. Everything that reaches a service beyond your TV and soundbar sits on one page. New ones start off, and Only free services turns them all off.
- **Artwork first**. The compact card leads with the artwork of what’s playing, and posters are shown whole.
- **Know what a long press does**. Holding a dock pin names its action in a bubble before it fires.
- **A new TV identity in one tap**. When the TV shows a second identity, you trust it in one tap instead of pairing again.

## Fixed

- **Holding an arrow**. Holding a d-pad arrow now holds it on the TV, without a backlog of repeats.
- **Typing on the TV**. Text sent to the TV arrives on time and in order, with the cursor after the last character.
- **Steady over Wi-Fi**. When Wi-Fi falters for a moment, tutti stays on it rather than trying the TV over mobile data and dropping the link.
- **An icon for every input**. The inputs sheet shows each input with its icon.
