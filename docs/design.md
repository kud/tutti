# Design direction (Iris)

The app replaces BRAVIA Connect day to day, with a Google TV–grade remote at its heart. Setup and room calibration stay in BRAVIA Connect.

## Principle: calm on top, deep underneath, and always say what unlocks more

Erwann, 2026-10-24: "Il faut vraiment que ce soit une app à la fois amazing UX et une app de geek dans les options. Assister aussi l'utilisateur pour lui dire ce qu'il doit faire s'il veut plus d'options, genre PSK etc."

- **The surface is calm.** The remote, the Son sheet and the scenes are for every evening: no protocol words, no ports, no readouts nobody asked for. A missing capability is never a dead control or a silent absence; it is one plain line saying why, with a way forward.
- **Depth lives one level down.** Everything an enthusiast wants (diagnostics, technical details, the readouts and hidden options the reverse map brings, `docs/reverse/bravia-connect.md`) sits in Settings › Advanced, behind developer mode. It may grow as large as it likes; it never leaks onto the everyday screens.
- **tutti always tells you what unlocks more, and how.** What tutti can do depends on setup done on the TV and in BRAVIA Connect. Each such step has one home, "What tutti can do" (below), that says whether it is done, what it unlocks, the exact menu path, and a button into tutti's own field for it. Wherever a missing step blocks something, the message that says so offers "Show me how", opening that step.

## Information architecture

Three places, opening on the remote.

To its right, against the chip, one "⋮" heard as "More options", at every width, whose menu has 48 dp text rows with their icons, "Scenes", "Edit dock" (the layout editor, Erwann, 2026-10-25), "Settings" then "Gestures"; then 24 dp of nothing; then Power at the far edge, so a thumb aimed at the menu can't land on Power (Erwann, 2026-10-25: « les étoiles aussi dans le menu », « le menu à côté du connecteur BRAVIA pour laisser de la place entre le bouton power », « les dots verticaux »; `POWER_GAP_DP`, `TopBarTest`). A long press (Erwann, 2026-10-24: « Appui long sur Power », « une modale qui propose quel device », « Bottom sheet plutôt que dialog ») opens a modal bottom sheet, `PowerSheet` through `TuttiSheet`, titled "Turn on or off", with three rows: "TV only", "Soundbar only", "Both", each with its glyph and the action in words from that device's own power ("Turn on", "Turn off", "Turn both off"), never a colour or a switch position (`powerChoices`, tested). A device whose power is unknown is offered "Turn on"; Both does exactly what the short press does. A sheet, not a dialog: it is a choice among actions like every other picker here, and it lands under the thumb. A tap runs the row and closes the sheet. The sheet opens with a TV set up, alone or with the bar (Iris's gesture audit, 2026-10-25); with the TV alone its row reads "The TV". Under the power rows, "Sleep timer" when the TV lists an IRCC sleep key (`Ircc.sleepTimerCode`): each tap sends one press of the TV's own key, which moves the TV to its next step and shows it on screen, so the sheet stays open for the next tap. TalkBack hears "Choose the TV, the soundbar or both" (TV alone: "Turn off the TV or set its sleep timer") as the long-press action. Rule: `sleepTimerRow` (`PowerSleepTest`).
   - Below: a horizontal strip of scene pills.
   - Under the pills, in the room the pad leaves: the now-showing card (Iris's option A, chosen by Erwann, 2026-10-24), centred in that room so the band reads as meant, never as a void. Full width less 16 dp margins: the app's icon (or the state's glyph), "On the TV", the name large and a small detail line. It says an app, a channel and programme, or an HDMI input (`nowShowing`), else "On the bar" and its source when the bar plays on its own (see "The bar on its own"), "TV on standby" (with "The bar stays on" when the bar plays), the home screen, or "TV out of reach"; never empty, and every state is told by its icon and words, never colour alone. Full is 104 dp tall and grows to 140 dp (larger icon and name) when the room allows, with at least 12 dp above and below; below that room it shrinks to one 56 dp line, and below that it hides (`placePad`, `cardFor`). While it shows, the system chip drops its "On the TV" line; hidden, the chip gets it back. While the TV shows an app, a channel or an input, a tap opens the playback sheet and, on an app, a long press its actions (see "Now-showing card: playback and app actions"); a quiet 20 dp chevron pointing up at the card's end, in onSurfaceVariant, says it opens, and TalkBack hears "Playback controls" and "App actions" as its actions. Home, standby, the bar on its own and out of reach don't open anything. State changes fade through by opacity only (the old words fade out before the new ones fade in, so two titles never overlap mid-change), which is also the reduced-motion form.
   - The pad mode switch (K1, Erwann, 2026-10-24): one quiet glyph on the pad's surface, in its top corner facing the rail (top-left for the right hand, top-right for the left), that leaves the pad's shape alone. It shows the mode a tap switches to, as that mode's outline icon (the touchpad's in D-pad mode, the D-pad's in touchpad mode), 24 dp, on-surface-variant, 16 dp in from the top and side edges, with no chip, border or label; the pad already says the current mode. A 48 dp hit area round it; pressed, the glyph tints and tightens. A still tap there switches with a select haptic and never reaches the TV as OK; a touch that starts there and moves is an ordinary swipe, and a swipe crossing it tracks as normal. One button to TalkBack: the state is the current mode, the action "Switch to touchpad" / "Switch to D-pad", with an action for each mode.

Right hand (the default, Erwann, 2026-10-24): pad on the right, rail on the left. Left hand: pad on the left, rail on the right. TalkBack reads the pad, then the rail, on either side. A 180 dp band along the pad's outer side, centred, keeps Android's back gesture off it (`padEdgeExclusion`, under the 200 dp per-edge budget), so a swipe from the pad's edge steps. Free room goes above the pad, as the now-showing card's room, never below the pad, and the pad never grows taller than its width (Compact 0.85, Large 1.2), so a tall screen doesn't stretch it upward (`placePad`). Touchpad or D-pad. Tap = OK. Long-press Select is reserved for the TV (sends a long press).

The stored order is the same for both hands (`RemoteLayout.rowOnScreen`). Mute is the volume rail's foot: the speaker there is a 48 dp key on a faint disc, the icon at full strength; a tap toggles mute, muted shows the slashed speaker over the hatched fill, and with the bar unreachable the rail is a hollow track and the speaker can't be tapped. The rail's drag starts only past touch slop, so a tap never moves the level, and a drag that starts on the speaker cancels the tap. The mic is hold to talk to the TV's assistant: it grows and fills with the primary colour while listening, a pill above it reads "À l'écoute… relâchez pour envoyer"; sliding a finger off turns it to the error container ("Relâchez pour annuler"), with a tick at the edge. A tap shorter than 300 ms sends nothing and explains the gesture. (Maya's placement, pending Iris's review.)
   - Beside the pad, on the side away from the thumb: vertical volume rail (soundbar volume, absolute). It always takes the pad's height, top and foot level with it, the same size and place in both modes (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "la taille du volume devrait être toujours la même, mais aussi D-pad et touchpad"; this reverses the same day's rail that stood in to the D-pad's disc). Drag anywhere on it to move the level. Three keys on it, one family (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "un bouton + et -, possiblement en haut et en bas"): + at the top, − just above the speaker, the mute speaker at the foot where it always was, each a 48 dp target on a solid 36 dp disc in the surface colour with a hairline ring and its icon at full strength, so it reads the same over the fill and the empty track ("pas ouf la transparence de l'icône"). + and − step the bar by one on press (a tick, the bump and the rail's stretch at a wall) and repeat on hold at the D-pad's rhythm; a drag that starts on a key still moves the rail. Muted: the speaker's slash, its disc inverted, the fill greyed and hatched. The level's number sits under + and always shows while the bar is connected ("je veux que le niveau de volume reste affiché"): the controller keeps the last level until the bar echoes a new one, live from the bar's notifies, + and − and drags; drawn twice and cut at the fill's edge (ink above, the fill's ink below), so a number the edge crosses stays legible; an ellipsis while the level is not reported yet (a press of + or − then asks the bar for it); no number while the bar is unreachable, never a stale level and never a dash that reads as the − key. Unreachable: a hollow track, no fill, the keys without discs and disabled, + and − dimmed, the speaker crossed. TalkBack: the rail is a slider (its value, set by swipe or volume keys), the keys are "Volume up", "Volume down" and "Mute". The number is a door (Iris's gesture audit, 2026-10-25): a tap on it (a 48 dp target between + and the fill) opens the volume sheet, and a quiet underline under the number says it opens; TalkBack hears it as an "Exact level" button and as an action on the rail. There is still no tap anywhere on the rail that jumps the level: a stray tap at 80 % at night is the one unforgivable remote mistake.
   - Bottom: the dock, Apps and Keys then his pins (see Shortcuts).
   - Peek sheet "Son" at the bottom; pulled up it shows sound controls.
2. **Scenes**: gallery and editor.
3. **Réglages**: devices and pairing (a TV but no bar: "Add the soundbar", the onboarding's Bar step alone, BRAVIA Connect's External control, the address and the three checks, then back to the remote, never through the TV's steps again (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "c'est dur de revenir sur le onboarding"); no TV: "Add a device", the whole flow; with a TV, "Run setup again" walks it all from the search), TV settings (picture, sound output, inputs, power saving), soundbar system settings, app preferences, haptics.

### What tutti can do (the setup checklist)

Settings' first row, "What tutti can do", reads the progress in words ("2 of 4 set up · next: IP control and PSK", or "All set up") and opens one screen (`ui/settings/UnlockScreen.kt`; the rules, pure and tested, in `Unlocks.kt` / `UnlocksTest`). A short intro ("The remote works as soon as the TV is paired…"), then one group per device:

- **TV**: *TV remote* (pairing, 6466/6467: the base), *IP control and PSK* (TV settings, what's playing and on which input, the TV's own apps and icons, waking a TV that is fully off, since IP control is where tutti learns its hardware address), *Remote start* (the TV stays reachable in standby, read from `getWolMode`).
- **Soundbar**: *Soundbar* (External control in BRAVIA Connect, TCP 33336: the rail, mute, the Son sheet, the bar in scenes, one Power).
- **For the curious**: *Developer mode*. Optional, outside the count.
- **Sony account**: *Sony sign-in*, "Coming later", nothing to do. Optional, outside the count; it becomes a real step when phase 2 of the reverse map lands.

Each step is one row: a status disc, the title, the status in a word, and what it unlocks in one small line under it. The status never rests on colour (Erwann is colourblind): each has its own glyph and word, done (filled disc, tick, "Done"), not yet (empty ring, "Not yet"), set up but failing (warning triangle, "Needs a look", plus "Now: PSK refused or missing" in the link's own words), checking (circling arrows), waiting on an earlier step (padlock, "Pair the TV first"), unknown (question mark, "Can't tell from here": Remote start before IP control answers), coming (timer). Only "Done" also fills its disc in the primary container, as emphasis on top of the tick.

One step is open at a time, and the screen opens on the one asked for (a nudge's) or else the next to do, failing steps before undone ones. Open, it shows "How" with the exact path on the TV or in BRAVIA Connect, numbered, and one button into tutti's own field: "Enter the PSK" (the PSK dialog in place), "Pair the TV", "Add the soundbar" (the onboarding's Bar step alone), "Turn on developer mode". Done steps keep a quiet way back ("Change the PSK", "Turn it off"); IP control done adds whether waking from off is ready. Menu paths are Sony's names in each language: English "Settings → Network & Internet → Local network setup → IP control", "Normal and Pre-Shared Key", "Remote start"; French "Paramètres → Réseau et Internet → Configuration du réseau local → Contrôle IP", « Normale et clé pré-partagée », « Démarrage à distance », and BRAVIA Connect's "Contrôle externe (External control)".

Nudges, where a missing step is the cause: a failed wake carries "Show me how" (no hardware address learnt → IP control; asleep or not found → Remote start; the bar silent → Soundbar), which takes the message's one action ahead of "Copy details"; the TV settings explainer adds "See everything tutti can do"; with no bar, the Son sheet and Settings already offer "Add the soundbar".

### Advanced

Settings' last technical group is "Advanced": the developer-mode switch, and with it on, Diagnostics and Technical details (`TechnicalScreen`: per device, the address, each link, whether a PSK is stored and whether the hardware address is learnt, never their values, Remote start, the model and generation, the features the bar refused; tutti's version). The reverse map's phase-2 readouts and hidden options join here as rows or screens of their own. Nothing in Advanced is needed day to day, and nothing from it appears elsewhere.

### Leaving setup

Opened from an app already set up (Settings' "Run setup again", "Add a device", "Add the soundbar", the Son sheet's or a checklist button), setup has a Back at the top left (48 dp, "Leave setup" to TalkBack), and the system back does the same: any pairing in progress is dropped and you land where you came from, nothing changed. Inside the flow, back from the code still steps back to the search first. The very first launch has no such exit: there is nowhere to go back to (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "il faudrait pas un bouton annuler ou retour ici ?").

One tap from the remote: power, volume, mute, input, keyboard, any scene, any shortcut, Night, Voice.
Apps have one door: the Dock's Apps; the TV's own apps screen is the first line of its sheet (Iris, 2026-10-24). All keys carries no apps and no "All apps" key: numbers and channels, colour keys, the TV keys, and Quick settings on the last row. The TV keys wear their glyph over their name (Erwann, 2026-10-25: « des icônes ici », « surtout sur la partie TV »), the one `keyIcon` gives that key everywhere (Menu is the options mark ⋮, kept apart from Guide's list), in tiles as tall as the colour keys; CH+ and CH− wear the channel arrows over "CH". Digits, Jump and TV stay words, the colour keys their swatch and name.

Two levels deep at most.

### "Son" sheet
Folded, it shows its handle and one line, "Sound" and what is on (with the bar on its own, the source first: "Sound · Bluetooth · Night"), and nothing else: the rest stays transparent and out of TalkBack until the sheet opens, then fades in by opacity (Erwann, 2026-10-24: the gesture bar's inset had let the top of "Bar input" show under the summary).

Sound field, Voice, Night, Auto volume, 360 Spatial (and Voice Zoom 3 when offered) as large toggle tiles: only real on/off settings are tiles. Each tile has its glyph top-start and its state mark top-end on one line, the name and its state under them, so the name keeps the tile's whole width (Iris, 2026-10-25). DRC and IMAX are three-way (Auto, On, Off), so each is a labelled row of three segments under the tiles, every option in view and the current one ticked and filled, read by TalkBack as the setting then each option as a radio button, as the bar settings screen lists the same choices (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "DRC est bizarre car c'est un toggle mais en fait il y a trois options"); Bass and Rear level as sliders; Voice Zoom 3 as a level slider only if the bar answers the capability probe. The rule is general (`soundSheetLayout`, tested): only a `Toggle` is a tile, every `Choice` gets all its options, a `Level` is a slider. Every documented value is listed, and a value the bar refuses (`ERR`/`NAK`) or never answers is rolled back to the confirmed one and remembered for the session (`TheatreState.refusedValues`): its segment, or its line in the bar settings menu, wears the warning glyph and the word "refused", the row says "The bar refused Auto. Tap it to try again.", and it stays tappable as a retry (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "tout lister et afficher une erreur quand l'option ne marche pas"). A greyed setting always shows its reason ("Voix désactivée : virtualiseur actif"), never hidden. "Recalibrer dans BRAVIA Connect" deep link. With no bar added, "Add the soundbar" sits under the title and opens the onboarding's Bar step alone.

"Bar input" sits above the tiles in the same dress as DRC and IMAX (Iris, 2026-10-24, after dev.75's chips wrapped AirPlay 2 onto a line of its own): its label with the current value on the right, then one outlined row of five segments, each a 20 dp icon over a `labelSmall` name (TV, HDMI 1, Spotify, Bluetooth, AirPlay). The chosen one is filled and its tick replaces its icon; Spotify wears its own mark, monochrome from Simple Icons as on the "On the bar" card (Erwann, 2026-10-25), and Bluetooth and AirPlay their symbols. A choice is optimistic like a tile (an arc past 150 ms, a refusal moves it back with reject and "Didn't take · try again" for 3 s); a refused input wears the warning glyph and the line under the row says "The bar refused …". TalkBack reads "Bar input", then each input as a radio button. With more than five inputs (the bar reporting one tutti doesn't know), the row scrolls sideways at a fixed segment width; it never wraps (`barInputScrolls`, tested).

Every section says what it does (Iris, 2026-10-24, from Erwann: "un moyen d'être explicatif sur chacune des options"): one quiet "?" (`ic_help`, 48 dp target, `onSurfaceVariant`) at the end of each section label, never an (i) per tile and never a long press, which nobody finds. The sections are Bar input, Effects (the tiles, under a label of their own), DRC, IMAX and Levels (Bass, Rear level, Voice Zoom level). The "?" opens a `TuttiSheet` titled with the section, its title pinned (`PinnedHeader`), with one or two plain sentences per setting, under each setting's name when the section holds several. The same sentence is each control's TalkBack hint, after its name and state (`explanation()` and `withHint()` in `SoundExplain.kt`, tested), so the two never drift. Strings live in `strings_explain.xml`. Accuracy is the rule: each line says only what Sony documents, and a claim that can't be confirmed is cut, not guessed (DRC's Auto says "when the source asks for it", not which formats; IMAX's Auto "leaves it to the bar"). TV settings get no "?".

### Sheets
Every sheet (Son, All keys, Apps, a pin's editor, the action picker) can rise to just below the status bar and no further: its handle and title always show, and what doesn't fit scrolls inside the sheet. Modal sheets go through `TuttiSheet` (`ui/common/Sheets.kt`).

The title (and a search, where there is one) stays put; only the body scrolls (Iris, 2026-10-24, from Erwann's dev.75 screenshots, where the Son sheet's "Sound" line and the picker's search slid half under the handle). `PinnedHeader` draws it: the Son sheet's "Sound · …" line, the action picker's title, search and filter chips, the Apps sheet's title and subtitle. Once the body has scrolled, a hairline in `outlineVariant` shows under the header, faded in by opacity (its own reduced-motion form). Folded, the Son sheet's peek is exactly its header, as before. The rule is general (Erwann, 2026-10-24: "chaque titre dans un bottom sheet devrait rester en haut et le reste scroll"): every sheet with a title pins it, Power, All keys and a pin's editor (whose Save stays in reach) included; a new sheet has a title and pins it.

Only the top moves a sheet (Erwann, 2026-10-25: « tout le contenu devrait scroller et pas que les boutons […] C'est juste principalement le haut qui devrait faire ça »; a drag between the Apps tiles slid the whole sheet when he meant to scroll). The handle and the pinned header drag it; everything below the header belongs to the body, gaps, padding and the space under a short list included: a drag there scrolls, or does nothing when there is nothing to scroll, and never moves the sheet, not even a pull-down at the top of the scroll. The scrim, back and the handle's own TalkBack actions still close a modal sheet, and the Son sheet's folded peek still opens from anywhere on it, since only downward drags are kept from the sheet. `PinnedHeader` holds the rule for every sheet; a full-height body (Apps, the action picker) fills down to the sheet's foot so the whole of it scrolls.

### Now-showing card: playback and app actions (Erwann, 2026-10-25)

Tap (« la lecture est pas mal »): a `TuttiSheet` whose pinned header is the card itself (icon, name, detail) under a small "Playback" label. Five round keys, previous, rewind, play/pause, fast forward, next, play/pause larger in the primary container; then two wide keys with their names, Subtitles and Audio track. Every one is a remote v2 media key sent through `sendTvKey` (`controller.key`), so a key pressed in a blip follows the TV link's rules: play/pause waits for the link, the rest are refused with their message (`TvLinkRule.HOLDABLE`). Only what is real: no source tells tutti whether an app is playing, its title or its position (remote v2 reports only the foreground package; `getPlayingContentInfo` answers for the tuner and inputs, not for apps), so play/pause is one toggle glyph that claims no state and there is no progress bar. The channel and programme in the header come from the TV's tuner when it reports them, as on the card. Rewind and fast forward, not "10 s": how far a press goes is the app's choice, and a quiet line says so ("Each app on the TV decides what these keys do"). With the TV link connecting or out of reach the keys wear the down dress, can't be pressed, and the reason sits above them in place of that line; power is not in this sheet. Every key is at least 56 dp and named to TalkBack; a press gives the key click. Rule and key list: `ui/remote/NowShowingActions.kt` (tested).

Long press, on an app only (« aller sur home de l'app ou kill l'app »): a `TuttiSheet` with the same header under "App actions", rows like the Power sheet's, offering only what tutti can do (`appActions`, tested): "Pin to the dock" (the Apps sheet's pin dialog) or "Remove from the dock" ("Frees pin n"); and, with a PSK, "Close apps on the TV", "Every app, not just this one", because Sony's IP control (`appControl.terminateApps`) closes every app at once and none by name. It asks first ("Close every app on the TV?"); with IP control not answering the row is shown dashed and disabled with why. Not offered: going back to the app's home, since a launch link on Google TV resumes the app where it was, and a store page, which tutti has never been seen to open. The long press is the card's only one; nothing else is assigned to it.

### Shortcuts
- Dock: four pins a page, up to three pages (swipe; dots only from page 2). The remote shows filled pins only, never an empty "+ Add" slot (Erwann, 2026-10-25: « un raccourci vers le layout directement dans les 3 dots », « on enlève les Add »): the dock is arranged from ⋮ › Edit dock (« Modifier le dock »), the same layout editor as Settings, where empty slots still show and take a pin, and "Pin to dock" still lists every slot, free ones as "empty". Fewer pins keep the one tile width a full row gives them (a sixth of the row, less gaps) and the row, doors and pins together, is centred as a group, never stretched: a stretched pin would change shape with every pin added, and a left-aligned row leaves a hole that reads as something missing (Iris). The group is as wide as the fullest page, so a swipe never moves the doors; a page with fewer pins starts against the doors, and a page with none is not swiped to (`shownPins`, `dockSpan`, `DockLayoutTest`). An empty dock shows one quiet text entry in the pins' place, "Customise the dock" (« Personnaliser le dock ») with the edit glyph, no tile fill, opening the same editor. Each pin has a name, an icon or two letters, a tap action (one action, or ordered steps with waits, tried from the editor with "Try") and its own long-press action, offered first in a menu above "Edit this pin".
- A pin whose tap toggles a bar setting wears that setting's state, live from the bar (Iris, 2026-10-24, after Erwann's "le bouton retient pas quand c'est activé ou non" about the default Night pin). The Sound tile's mark at badge size, on the icon's top-end corner and cut out of it by a ring of the tile's colour: on is a filled disc with a check, and the tile itself takes the accent fill and squares its corners from 20 to 14 dp, the remote's one "on" dress (Visual, State); off is an empty ring on the plain tile; not known is a dashed ring in the outline tone, never an empty "off" ring: the bar connected but silent on it, the bar unreachable (a remembered value is not shown once the link drops) or no bar added. A tap shows the asked value at once (optimistic); past 150 ms unconfirmed a thin arc turns round the badge (still under reduced motion), and a refusal springs it back, shakes the pin 4 dp and plays reject, as the Sound tile does (the haptic alone under reduced motion). The fill changes by opacity, the corners by spring (a snap under reduced motion). TalkBack hears a switch with its state: "On", "Off", or why it is unknown ("Not reported by the bar", "Bar 9 unreachable", "No soundbar added"). The state follows the tap action only: a long-press toggle shows "On" or "Off" beside its line in the pin's menu (nothing while unknown), never on the face. A pin that sets a value or runs steps has no state. Rule in `pinToggle` (`ui/remote/PinToggle.kt`, tested).
- A toggle pin pressed with no bar to answer (unreachable or not added) sends nothing: the Sound tile's bump, its dashed badge saying why. Connected but unreported, it sends On: a pin is pressed to turn its setting on, and a refusal springs it back (Maya, 2026-10-24; `TheatreState.toggleTarget`).
- Long-press on any dock slot or secondary button (Home, Back, Play) assigns a second action.
- A key is pressed; holding is chosen per step in the pin's editor, never in the list (Iris, 2026-10-24: the picker's short/held pills on every row read as noise, and "held" meant nothing until it was pinned). The action picker picks a press. In the editor, a key step, and a key as the pin's long-press action, has a quiet "Press" / "Hold" pair under it (« Appuyer » / « Maintenir »), Press by default, read as radio buttons; a pin saved with a held key shows "Hold". Every key in the picker wears its own glyph: rewind, fast forward, captions, audio track and the other playback keys no longer borrow play/pause (`KeyIconTest`).
- Pad gestures, two only: two-finger tap = play/pause; two-finger vertical drag = bar volume.

### Edit mode
- Settings › Shortcuts › "Remote layout", or "Edit the remote layout" at the foot of a pin's editor, turns the remote into its own edit mode: the same screen, what can change in a dashed frame, what can't under a lock. Changes save as they are made; "Done" (or Back) leaves.
- Adjustable: the round-button row (up to six, from Back, Home, Play/Pause, Voice, Keyboard, Info, Subtitles, Input, Guide, TV settings; drag sideways or TalkBack's move left / right), the scene strip (shown or hidden), the pad in three steps (Compact is shorter, Normal is as tall as it is wide, Large takes the margins back and stands taller; the rail follows the pad's height in each), the hand (Left / Right, one switch; it mirrors the pad, the rail and the round-button row, never Power, the scenes, the dock or the Son sheet; a move in the editor lands where it was dropped on either hand), and the pins' order within a page (drag, or move left / right; tap opens the pin's editor).
- Anchors never move or hide: Power (short press both, long press the device sheet), the volume rail, the pad's place and the Son sheet. The pad's side is chosen once, by the hand, never dragged; the rail takes the other side. "Reset the layout" asks first and puts the row, the scenes, the pad and the hand (right) back; pins stay.
- Stored with every field defaulted to today's screen, so a phone that never opens edit mode sees no change.

## Gestures: the long-press grammar and the discoverability rule (Erwann, 2026-10-25)

**Grammar.** Tap does; long press opens the thing's menu (first row the most useful, last "Pin to dock" or "Edit"); drag sets a continuous value. No double tap anywhere: it would delay every OK by about 300 ms. When the menu would have a single row (an effect tile, a DRC or IMAX choice, a key in All keys, a TV setting value), the long press goes straight to that row: "Pin to dock" opens the slot dialog.

**"Pin to dock" everywhere it makes sense.** Effect tiles, bar inputs, DRC and IMAX choices, All keys, TV settings values: one `DockPinner` (`ui/remote/PinAnywhere.kt`) writes the dock's own model (`DockAction`), so the pin is like any other.

**Bar inputs.** Long press a segment: Pair a device (Bluetooth only, first), Rename (tutti only), Hide (never the input playing now), Pin to dock. Hidden inputs come back from Settings › Soundbar › Hidden inputs.

**Launcher.** Long press on tutti's icon: the first three scenes in his order, then Keyboard; kept in step with the scenes whenever the app is in front.

**Discoverability: a hidden gesture exists only if all five hold.**

1. It shows it is coming: the ring sweeps the control's edge from 150 ms to the long-press timeout (`holdForMenu` for controls that keep their own tap, `longPressRing` on the round keys), and the menu, dialog or tag names what it did.
2. It is listed in the registry, `ui/common/Gestures.kt`, the single source of truth, which the remote's top bar shows as the "Gestures" sheet: the "Gestures" row of the "⋮" menu, after "Scenes" and "Settings" (Erwann, 2026-10-25; `TopBarTest`). A gesture not in the registry does not ship.
3. At most one hint per gesture, once, at the right moment; never two the same day; showing is spending, so a dismissal is permanent (`GestureHints`, `TuttiController.takeHint`).
4. Never only behind a gesture: each action also has a visible way in (a menu, a sheet, Settings).
5. TalkBack first: every long press is a `CustomAccessibilityAction` named by what it does, never "long press"; state never rests on colour; 48 dp targets; a TV or bar control whose link is down refuses its long press too (a bump), except Power.

## The bar on its own

Erwann, 2026-10-24: no switch, a state that comes and goes by itself. The rule is `barAlone` (`ui/remote/BarAlone.kt`, tested in `BarAloneTest`).

- **When.** The TV is in standby, or out of reach, while the bar is on, answering, and playing one of its own sources (`main.input` bluetooth, airplay2 or spotify). It ends by itself when the TV wakes or the input goes back to tv or hdmi1. The bar on "tv" with the TV asleep stays plain standby ("TV on standby · The bar stays on").
- **Card.** "On the bar" over the source's name large, with its glyph (Spotify's mark, the Bluetooth and AirPlay symbols), and "TV on standby" small under it, or "TV out of reach" when the TV's power is unknown. Glyph and words, never colour. It outranks standby and out of reach on the card, since it says what is actually playing.
- **"Turn on the TV".** The card's one button, only in this state: the power glyph and the words ("Turn on" on the 56 dp line, the full label to TalkBack). It wakes the TV alone through the same path as the Power sheet's "TV only" and leaves the bar alone. HDMI-CEC may still switch the bar to the TV input when the TV wakes: unverified on the device. Disabled while a wake is under way.
- **Chip.** With the TV known asleep, no "unreachable" and no "Connecting…": "BRAVIA 8 on standby", quiet. Out of reach with its power unknown keeps today's words.
- **Pad.** A press while the TV is asleep sends nothing: the reject haptic and "Not sent: the TV is on standby", with "Turn on" as the message's action. The volume rail and its keys are the bar's and work as ever. Power keeps its usual press; the card's button and the long press wake the TV alone.
- **Son sheet.** Its folded line names the source first. « Et la feuille Son peut monter toute seule » (Erwann, 2026-10-24): when the state starts, the sheet rises once by itself, its controls being the ones that matter now. Folded again, it stays folded until the state ends; coming back to the remote doesn't raise it a second time. It never rises over the keyboard view or edit mode.

## The TV's link: calm, then honest

Iris, 2026-10-24, from Erwann's "si les touches ne marchent pas à ce moment-là, peut-être que c'est mieux". The rule is `TvLinkRule` (`controller/TvLinkRule.kt`, tested); the remote reads its result, `Links.tvShown`, and Technical details keep the raw link.

- Before: every drop of the remote v2 link showed at once (chip "BRAVIA 8 unreachable" in red, then "Connecting…", the card on "TV out of reach"), even for a one-second blip; and a key pressed meanwhile was dropped by `AtvRemote.send` with nothing said and nothing felt beyond the press's own click.
- A blip shows nothing. For 3 s after a link that was up drops, the remote stays as it was, the last app included. A reconnect is a 1 s backoff, then TLS and the configure exchange: about 1.5 to 2.5 s on a home LAN, so 3 s covers the first retry with margin.
- Past 3 s, quietly: the chip's dot becomes the open ring with "Reconnecting to the TV…" (onSurfaceVariant, never the error colour), and the card says "Reconnecting…" over "Keys work again once the TV answers", with the turning-arrow glyph, not the warning triangle.
- Past 20 s (the 1, 2, 4 and 8 s backoffs and their attempts), the stronger state comes back: "BRAVIA 8 unreachable" in the chip, "TV out of reach" on the card, the down dress on every TV control (Visual, State). A retry after that never flickers back to "Connecting…". A link never up this session (the first connect), Needs pairing and no TV show as they are, with their own messages and actions.
- A link that is down wins on the card over a channel or input that REST still reports: the card vouches for the keys. Standby still wins over everything.
- From 3 s on (reconnecting, then out of reach) every TV control wears the down dress and a tap sends nothing (Visual, State), so the rules below apply to a key pressed inside the blip, or sent from elsewhere (a widget, a scene).
- A TV key pressed while the link isn't up never vanishes. One-shot keys whose effect doesn't depend on focus (Home, Back, play/pause, play, pause, stop, Guide, Settings, All apps, TV) wait up to 2 s and go the moment the link is back, newest wins: a soft bump and "Sending when the TV is back…". Everything else is dropped with the reject haptic and "Not sent: waiting for the TV": D-pad and OK (a burst of arrows landing late, or OK on whatever took focus), digits and channel steps, colour keys, skips, long presses, and power, which has its own wake path. A held key that doesn't make it says "Not sent: the TV didn't come back in time". Past give-up it is "Not sent: the TV is out of reach", and with pairing lost "Not sent: pair the TV again". The same words don't repeat while they still show, so a burst of arrows is one message and a reject per press.
- The bar's keys (volume, mute) are their own link and never wait on the TV's.
- TalkBack hears the change once, when the shown state moves: "Reconnecting to the TV…", "BRAVIA 8 unreachable", then "Connected to the TV again"; never on a blip, never at the first connect. The card's change is its usual opacity fade-through, which is also the reduced-motion form; the ring stops turning under reduced motion, as before.

## Scenes (templates)
- A scene holds any subset of TV settings (input or app, picture mode), bar settings (sound field, voice, night, bass, volume, optional volume cap) and a final action (launch an app).
- Main creation path: "Capturer l'état actuel" — every captured value becomes a removable chip; name, colour and glyph. Blank form is the fallback.
- Per-app profiles: "Quand Netflix est au premier plan, appliquer Cinéma" (while connected).
- Strip: pill in its own tonal colour. Gallery: large card with plain-words summary ("Atmos · Voix + · Basses −2"). Active scene filled; manual change → outline marked "modifiée". Applying fills the pill left to right (progress).
- Starter gallery: Cinéma, Série du soir, Nuit (volume cap 20), Musique, Sport, Jeu, Info.
- Long press on a pill opens its menu (Iris's gesture audit, 2026-10-25), the most useful first: "Undo the last scene" on the pill that can still be undone, then "Apply", "Edit scene", "Pin to the dock". Each row is also a TalkBack action on the pill (`sceneMenu`, tested).
- Undo the last scene: just before a scene runs, tutti keeps what it is about to change, only the keys it touches, as they stand (`SceneUndo`, `:protocol`, tested), and the scene that was active. After a clean run the message "Cinéma applied" carries "Undo"; the pill's menu keeps it. Undoing restores those values through the same runner (never waking or switching anything off) and makes the previous scene active again; the message says "Cinéma undone", or the problem if some steps failed. One level: a new scene replaces it, undoing spends it. Stale, and no longer offered, after 15 minutes, once another scene is active (or this one is deleted), or once the room is switched off.

## Volume sheet, cap for tonight, high-volume guard (Erwann, 2026-10-25)

The rail drives the soundbar, `main.volumestep` on its own 0–100 scale (`TheatreFeature.VOLUME`, `MAX_VOLUME`), so the sheet, the cap and the guard are all on that scale. The TV's level only reaches the quick surfaces with no bar configured (through remote v2 keys, on the TV's own `0..max`); the guard and the cap don't apply there, since the app has no TV volume control to confirm from.

- **Volume sheet** (tap the rail's number, which wears the Now showing card's quiet up-chevron under it, never an underline, which read as a web link: Erwann, 2026-10-25, « pas sûr du souligné »; `VolumeSheet`, a `TuttiSheet` with a pinned "Volume" header and the bar's name). Exact level: −, + and a slider move a *new level* drawn large, "Now 18" above it; only "Set to n" sends it, never a stray touch. Three saved levels: a filled tile shows its level and a tap sets it; an empty one, dashed, reads "Save n here"; a long press offers "Replace with n" and "Remove" (TalkBack actions too). Both go through the controller like the rail, so the ceiling clamps them and the guard asks above its line. With the bar down the level and the saved levels can't be sent and the reason shows with the warning glyph; the cap can still be set.
- **Cap for tonight**: a ceiling until a time (1 hour, 3 hours, or until 7:00 the next morning), stored in prefs (`TonightCap`, wall clock) and dropped when it passes. It is enforced exactly like a scene's cap: the ceiling is the lower of the two (`VolumeRules.ceiling`), every surface clamps to it, the rail draws its dashed line, and a scene's volume step is clamped to it too. Setting it brings a louder bar down at once.
- **High-volume guard**: on by default at 30 on the bar's 0–100 scale (Erwann: « 30 ou 40, il faudrait étudier, mais 30 c'est pas mal »; about a third of the travel, already loud for a Bar 9 at night), adjustable in Settings › Volume (5 to 99, in steps of one) or off. A change that would take the bar from at or under the line to above it stops exactly on the line (`VolumeRules.decide`, tested): repeat-on-hold, fast taps and drags never overshoot while the question is pending, because each press is judged from the newest level asked, not a lagging state. In the app (rail, + and −, the pad's two fingers, the sheet, the phone's keys while the remote is in front) a dialog asks « Attention, le volume devient élevé » / "Go above 30? The volume will go to 45." with "Go to 45" and "Stay at 30"; dismissing stays. Confirmed, it doesn't nag: the bar may go above until its level is under the line again, the bar's link drops, or the app restarts. A level already above the line (the bar's own remote) is not a crossing. Where there is no room to ask (widget, notification and its volume keys, Quick Settings overlay) it stops on the line and says so: "max 30 without the app" under the widget's number, "Above 30: open tutti" in the notification's line and the overlay's status, and the overlay's drag stops there. The cap wins: the guard sits under it and does nothing when the cap is at or below its line. Scenes, and undoing one, are not stopped: running a scene is an explicit choice of its volume (the ceiling still applies).

- Posts are held 400 ms apart, trailing, and an unchanged card is never re-posted: Android silently sheds updates past about five a second, and the shed one can be the last.

  - Gauges (the volume rail's fill, a scene's progress) are amounts, not states.
  - Down has one dress across the remote (Iris, 2026-10-25, from Erwann's "pourquoi quand c'est pas connecté et en train de connecter le statut des boutons n'est pas le même, et surtout ce ne sont pas tous les boutons ?" and "non connecté ou en cours de connexion ça devrait être pareil, genre disabled"). A control whose device's link isn't up, connecting or out of reach alike, loses its fill and wears a dashed ring in the outline tone, its glyph in onSurfaceVariant (`drawDownRing`): the round keys, Keyboard, the mic, the Dock's pins. The pad keeps its surface, so the big area reads as waiting rather than broken: a dashed edge runs round it and its chevrons, OK and mode switch dim to 38%. Shape says it, never colour alone; it changes by opacity, which is also its reduced-motion form. A tap on a down control bumps, nudges the system chip (which says why) and sends nothing; a down pin's long press still opens its menu and the editor. TalkBack reads the reason as the state: "Connecting to the TV" or "BRAVIA 8 unreachable" (the bar's: "Connecting to the bar", "Bar 9 unreachable"); the pad, down, is one node with no actions. Keyboard never wears its "on" look while down: a field remembered from before the drop is not a field now. All keys follows it too (Erwann, 2026-10-25, from a dev screenshot whose bar said "Connecting…" over a sheet of live-looking keys): every key in it is a TV key, so with the TV down every key wears the dress, the colour swatches dim to 38%, the reason heads the sheet as on the playback sheet, a tap bumps and sends nothing, and the long press refuses "Pin to dock" with a bump, as the Sound sheet's pins do while the bar is down.
  - Per device, never "anything down disables everything": TV controls follow the TV's shown link (`TvLinkRule`, so a blip under 3 s stays normal), bar controls (Mute, the bar's pins) follow the bar's. With the TV out of reach and the bar connected, volume, Mute, the Sound sheet and the bar's pins stay normal. Power, scenes and macros never go down: they wake the TV, which is exactly when it is out of reach, and a scene runs its bar steps whatever the TV does. Power pins count as Power. A device not added has nothing to wait for and stays normal. Rule in `controlLink` and `dockOwner` (`ui/remote/ControlLink.kt`, tested).
- Performance target: visual and haptic response within one frame of a tap, before any network round trip.
- UI in English by default (`values/`), French as a translation (`values-fr/`); every string in both.

## Words: one name per thing (Iris, 2026-10-25)

Each thing has one name in each language, everywhere: screens, TalkBack, the notification and the widget.

| Thing | English | French |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The television | TV (never "television") | la TV (never "téléviseur", "télé") |
| The soundbar | soundbar where it is named alone (titles, TalkBack, notifications); "the bar" in running text once it is on screen | barre de son alone; « la barre » in running text |
| A dock button the user sets | pin (never "shortcut", "button") | épingle (never « raccourci », « bouton ») |
| TV applications | apps | apps (never « applis », « applications ») |
| Power | Power for the control; Turn on / Turn off for the action | Marche / arrêt for the control (never « Alimentation », « Power »); Allumer / Éteindre for the action |
| The TV's input | Input | Entrée (never « Source ») |

- IP control has one menu path, written the same in every string: Settings → Network & Internet → Local network setup → IP control (FR: Paramètres → Réseau et Internet → Configuration du réseau local → Contrôle IP).
- Link states in French are invariable (« en ligne », « injoignable », « à configurer »), so they agree with any device: « télécommande en ligne », « barre injoignable ».
- Counts use plurals in both languages; French `one` covers 0 and 1.
- Failure marks are `ic_warning`, never ✕: ✕ means close or remove.
- Every choice in a picker is a 48 dp target with a name TalkBack reads; a chosen colour also wears a tick.
- Landscape is supported, not locked: the app pads the display cutout's sides, so no control sits under a camera hole. Indeterminate bars (`BusyBar`) stand still at a third under reduced motion, like the spinners' still arc.
- The Forget dialog names the device it forgets.
